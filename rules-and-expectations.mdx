Every member of Project 909 is expected to uphold the standards outlined in this document from their very first patrol. These rules exist to protect the quality of roleplay for everyone in the community and to ensure that Project 909 remains a professional, immersive environment. Read this page in full before your first session — not knowing the rules is not a valid defence when they are violated.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="General Conduct">
    Project 909 holds all members to a high standard of conduct both in-game and within community spaces such as Discord.

    **In-Game Conduct**
    - Roleplay every situation realistically and in good faith. Treat other players — whether they are community members or civilians — with the same professionalism you would expect in return.
    - Do not break character during active roleplay scenarios. Use the appropriate out-of-character channels in Discord for non-roleplay communication.
    - Do not exploit game mechanics, use glitches, or engage in any behaviour that undermines the integrity of a scene.
    - Conduct involving excessive force, unlawful arrests, or unrealistic tactics must be justifiable and in line with your department's use-of-force policy.

    **Off-Duty Conduct**
    - Treat all community members with respect in Discord and other shared spaces. Harassment, discrimination, and personal attacks will not be tolerated regardless of context.
    - Do not publicly air internal disputes or criticise staff decisions in open channels. Raise concerns through the appropriate ticket or private channel.

    **Activity Requirements**
    - All active members are expected to participate in at least one patrol session per week.
    - Members assigned to specialized units must complete a minimum of **three patrol sessions per week** to retain their division placement.
    - Extended absences must be reported to your supervisor using the leave of absence process in Discord. Unreported inactivity for 14 or more days may result in removal from your division or rank.

    <Warning>
      Failure to meet activity requirements for specialized units without an approved leave of absence will result in removal from that unit. Repeated inactivity without notice may lead to a rank reduction or removal from the community.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Uniform Standards">
    Correct uniform presentation is required at all times while on duty. Your uniform identifies your department, rank, and assignment — wearing incorrect or unauthorized clothing undermines the realism of the community.

    - Always wear the full, approved uniform for your department and rank. Partial uniforms, incorrect rank insignia, or unauthorized modifications are not permitted.
    - Uniform loadouts are defined per department. Check your department's uniform guide in Discord for the exact outfit codes and permitted variations.
    - Plain-clothes or undercover uniforms are only permitted for members assigned to an investigative division and only when actively operating in that capacity.
    - Off-duty attire may be worn only when you are explicitly off duty and not responding to any active incident or call.
    - Do not modify your character's appearance in a way that conflicts with a professional law enforcement standard — this includes unrealistic hairstyles, visible tattoos on the face or neck, and non-regulation accessories.

    <Note>
      If you are unsure whether a specific uniform item is permitted, ask your supervisor or check the pinned uniform reference in your department's Discord channel before wearing it on patrol.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Radio Discipline">
    Radio communication is one of the most visible aspects of your professionalism in Project 909. All members are expected to maintain clear, concise, and correct radio discipline at all times while on duty.

    - Use only approved ten-codes and plain language when communicating over the radio. Slang, jokes, and unnecessary transmissions clutter the channel and create confusion during active scenes.
    - Identify yourself by your callsign at the start of every transmission. Do not broadcast without first stating who you are and what channel you are on.
    - Keep transmissions brief. State your unit, your message, and your location. Wait for acknowledgement before continuing.
    - Do not talk over other units. If the channel is busy, wait for a break before transmitting unless you have an emergency.
    - Out-of-character conversation must never take place on the radio. Use Discord voice channels or direct messages for anything that is not in-character.

    <Tip>
      Before your first patrol, review the radio usage guide and ten-codes reference so your transmissions are accurate from day one. Supervisors notice clean radio work immediately.
    </Tip>

    <Warning>
      Repeated radio violations — including in-character profanity, unauthorized channel use, or consistently poor radio discipline — are treated as a conduct issue and may result in a formal warning or temporary suspension from duty.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Vehicle Usage">
    Department vehicles are a privilege, not a right. All members are expected to operate assigned vehicles in a realistic manner consistent with law enforcement standards.

    - Only operate vehicles that are approved for your rank and assignment. Patrol units use standard patrol vehicles. Unmarked, specialty, or divisional vehicles require the appropriate role or authorization.
    - Obey all in-game traffic laws unless actively responding to a priority call with emergency equipment activated. Even with lights and siren, you are expected to exercise reasonable care.
    - Do not ram, pit, or use your vehicle as a weapon outside of a lawful pursuit scenario. Any vehicle contact must be justifiable under your department's pursuit and use-of-force policy.
    - Park your vehicle appropriately when on scene — blocking traffic unnecessarily, parking on sidewalks, or leaving vehicles unsecured in active scenes is not permitted.
    - If your assigned vehicle is damaged during a scene, document it in Imperial CAD and report it to your supervisor. Do not continue a patrol in a visually wrecked or smoking vehicle.

    <Note>
      Pursuit policy is governed by a separate document available in your department's section. All members assigned to highway and motor units must read and acknowledge the pursuit policy before operating on patrol.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Rank Progression

Rank advancement in Project 909 is based on demonstrated performance, consistent activity, and supervisor recommendation. There is no automatic time-in-rank promotion — you earn your next rank by showing up, doing the job well, and receiving an endorsement from your chain of command.

The general rank progression for patrol members is:

| Rank | Description |
|---|---|
| **Constable** | Entry-level rank. All new members begin here after completing FTO. |
| **Senior Constable** | Awarded after demonstrated consistent patrol performance and conduct. |
| **Leading Senior Constable** | Eligible to apply for specialized divisions and training roles. |
| **Detective / Specialist** | Assigned to an investigative or specialized unit following a formal application. |
| **Sergeant** | First supervisory rank. Responsible for overseeing patrol units and junior members. |
| **Senior Sergeant and above** | Command-level roles appointed by department leadership. |

Supervisors submit promotion recommendations through the appropriate Discord channel. Promotions are announced by department leadership and take effect immediately upon announcement.

## Consequences for Violations

Violations of community rules are handled through a structured discipline process. The severity of the response depends on the nature and frequency of the violation.

- **Verbal Warning** — issued for minor first-time violations. Documented internally by the supervisor.
- **Written Warning** — issued for repeated minor violations or a single moderate violation. Logged in the member's record.
- **Suspension from Duty** — issued for serious violations or following multiple written warnings. Duration is set by department leadership.
- **Demotion** — applied when conduct or performance no longer reflects the member's current rank. May accompany or follow a suspension.
- **Removal from Community** — reserved for the most serious violations, including repeated misconduct, harassment, or intentional disruption of community operations.

<Warning>
  Certain violations — including harassment of other members, exploitation of game mechanics, sharing of private community materials, and impersonation of staff — are treated as serious offences and may result in immediate removal from the community without prior warning.
</Warning>

All discipline decisions can be appealed through the staff ticket system in Discord. Appeals must be submitted within 72 hours of the disciplinary action being issued.
