The Highway Safety Division (HSD) is a specialized unit dedicated to traffic enforcement, proactive policing, and highway patrol across San Bernardino County. As an HSD member, you operate both marked siderunner units and low-visibility ghosted vehicles to deter dangerous driving, respond to traffic incidents, and maintain a consistent enforcement presence on the county's road network. Professionalism and realistic traffic roleplay are the cornerstones of everything HSD does.

## Division Overview

HSD exists to bridge the gap between general patrol and dedicated highway law enforcement. Your primary focus as an HSD constable is the road — monitoring traffic flow, conducting enforcement stops, responding to collisions, and using proactive tactics to reduce dangerous driving behaviour before incidents occur. Ghosted units allow you to blend into regular traffic for targeted enforcement without the deterrent effect of a fully marked cruiser.

## Responsibilities

- Conduct traffic enforcement stops and issue citations for moving violations
- Respond to traffic collisions, road hazards, and highway emergencies
- Perform proactive highway patrols to deter dangerous driving behaviour
- Operate ghosted units for low-visibility enforcement and targeted traffic operations
- Coordinate with frontline patrol units during major incidents affecting highway corridors
- Maintain detailed and accurate reports for all stops, citations, and incidents
- Enforce all relevant traffic and highway statutes in a realistic and professional manner

## Qualifications

- Must hold the rank of **HSD Constable** or higher
- Authorized by HSD leadership prior to operating any HSD-designated vehicle
- Must demonstrate consistent, realistic traffic enforcement roleplay
- Supervisor approval required before conducting extended or specialized operations

## Rules and Restrictions

Follow these rules at all times while operating as an HSD member. Violations may result in removal from the division.

- **HSD vehicles are restricted to HSD members only.** Non-HSD personnel may not operate siderunners or ghosted units without explicit authorization.
- **Ghosted units are for proactive enforcement only.** Do not use them as a substitute for standard marked patrol.
- **Maintain realistic traffic enforcement RP.** All stops, citations, and responses must reflect real-world law enforcement procedures.
- **Supervisor approval is required for extended operations.** Long-duration or specialized deployments require sign-off from your supervisor before proceeding.
- Uniform must remain **clean and professional** at all times — no unauthorized clothing or accessories.
- Your uniform must match your **HSD role and current assignment**.

<Warning>
  Non-HSD members found operating Highway Safety Division vehicles without authorization may be removed from active patrol. HSD members found misusing ghosted units or operating out of uniform may face disciplinary action or removal from the division.
</Warning>

## How to Apply

Speak with an HSD supervisor or command member in-game or through the community Discord to express your interest in joining the Highway Safety Division. Demonstrate consistent patrol activity, professional traffic enforcement RP, and a strong understanding of highway law before submitting your interest.

<Tip>
  Build your reputation on patrol first. HSD leadership looks for members who already enforce traffic realistically before they put on the HSD patch.
</Tip>
