The K9 Division is a specialized unit pairing certified handlers with trained police canines to support suspect tracking, area searches, and high-risk operations across San Bernardino County. As a K9 handler, you bring a critical capability to the department that no other unit can replicate — your canine partner is a force multiplier in the field, but that power comes with serious responsibility. Handlers are accountable for every deployment, every bite, and every moment their dog is working.

## Division Overview

K9 operations are activated when ground units require enhanced tracking ability, building or area searches, suspect apprehension assistance, or tactical support during high-risk incidents. You and your canine partner operate as a team at all times. Proper deployment, realistic use-of-force decisions, and strict handler accountability are what separate a professional K9 unit from an irresponsible one. Every K9 callout must be justifiable and documented.

## Responsibilities

- Deploy canines for suspect tracking and apprehension following foot pursuits or known suspect locations
- Conduct building searches, area sweeps, and evidence recovery operations
- Support ERT and frontline patrol during high-risk incidents requiring a K9 capability
- Ensure all K9 deployments follow established use-of-force guidelines
- Maintain positive control of your canine at all times on and off active deployment
- Complete accurate and detailed reports for every K9 callout and deployment
- Maintain the K9 vehicle and specialist equipment to operational standards

## Qualifications

- Must be a **certified K9 handler** — authorization from K9 leadership is required before operating any K9 unit
- Must demonstrate realistic, justified, and proportionate K9 deployment decisions
- Must have a thorough understanding of **K9 use-of-force policy** before deploying in the field
- Strong communication and report-writing skills required
- Responsible for your canine's behaviour and actions at all times

## Rules and Restrictions

These rules govern all K9 operations. As a handler, you are personally responsible for every decision made with your canine partner.

- **K9 vehicles are restricted to certified handlers only.** Unauthorized use of a K9 vehicle is a direct policy violation.
- **Every K9 deployment must be realistic and justified.** Deploying your canine without a lawful, proportionate reason is a use-of-force violation.
- **Handlers are responsible for K9 control at all times.** If your canine causes unnecessary harm or is deployed improperly, the responsibility falls on you.
- **K9 use must follow all use-of-force guidelines.** Treat every K9 deployment with the same gravity as any other use-of-force decision.
- Maintain the green ERT uniform at all times while on K9 patrol duty.
- Document every deployment — a K9 callout without a report is an incomplete one.

<Warning>
  Unauthorized K9 use, improper deployments, or failure to maintain the correct uniform may result in **immediate removal from the K9 Unit**. K9 capability is a privilege, not a right.
</Warning>

## How to Apply

Approach a K9 supervisor or command member in-game or through the community Discord to express your interest in becoming a certified K9 handler. Demonstrate strong patrol fundamentals, professional roleplay, and a clear understanding of use-of-force principles before putting your name forward.

<Tip>
  K9 leadership wants handlers who understand restraint as much as deployment. Show that you know *when not* to use K9 — that's just as important as knowing when to deploy.
</Tip>
