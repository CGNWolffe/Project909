The Air Operations Division (AOD) is the department's aviation capability — a specialized unit that extends the reach of every ground operation through aerial patrol, real-time tactical support, search and rescue missions, and drone-based surveillance. As an AOD operator, you provide a perspective no ground unit can match: the ability to track fleeing suspects across terrain, monitor large-scale incidents from above, locate missing persons, and deliver aerial overwatch for events that put large numbers of people in one place. This division demands professionalism, precision, and a genuine commitment to aviation and operational safety.

## Division Overview

AOD operators serve two distinct roles — pilots who fly rotary-wing aircraft and ground drone operators who deploy UAV systems for tactical and investigative support. Both roles are mission-critical. Pilots provide direct aerial engagement: pursuits, extractions, reconnaissance, and search and rescue. Drone operators extend surveillance capability without committing an aircraft, supporting CIB investigations, tactical operations, and incident command with real-time aerial feeds. Both roles operate under strict safety and deployment standards — the aircraft and drone systems you control are expensive, complex, and powerful assets that must be treated accordingly.

## Responsibilities

- Provide aerial support to SBCSD and CHP ground operations across San Bernardino County
- Conduct aerial patrol and highway monitoring to assist frontline enforcement
- Assist ground units during vehicle pursuits and high-risk incidents requiring airborne tracking
- Operate drone systems during tactical operations, CIB investigative support, and incident overwatch
- Conduct search and rescue missions in coordination with ground search teams
- Provide aerial overwatch at public events, large-scale incidents, and sensitive operations
- Support ERT and CIB operations with airborne reconnaissance and tactical coordination
- Maintain aircraft and drone systems to operational readiness standards at all times
- Successfully complete all required flight and operational evaluations before independent deployment

## Qualifications

<Steps>
  <Step title="Meet the Rank Requirement">
    You must hold the rank of **Senior Constable or higher** to be eligible for AOD. The rank requirement ensures you bring solid patrol and communication experience to aviation operations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Meet the Activity Standard">
    AOD requires a minimum of **3 days of active operation per week**. Aviation assets must be available consistently — the division cannot function with irregular operators.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Demonstrate Communication and Professionalism">
    Strong communication skills are essential in aviation operations. You must demonstrate professionalism, operational awareness, and the ability to perform calmly under pressure during every deployment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Flight and Operational Evaluations">
    All AOD candidates must complete the **AOD training and evaluation process**, including flight evaluations for pilots and drone qualification assessments for ground operators. There are no shortcuts — you must be cleared before operating any AOD asset independently.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Rules and Restrictions

AOD operates under strict safety and operational standards. Every rule below exists to protect operators, assets, and the integrity of the community's aviation operations.

- **Aircraft and drone units are restricted to authorized AOD personnel only.** Unauthorized operation of any AOD asset is a direct and serious policy violation.
- **Aircraft may only be deployed for authorized operational purposes.** Personal flights, joy rides, and unauthorized sorties are strictly prohibited.
- **Unauthorized stunting or reckless flying is strictly prohibited.** Every flight must be conducted with the safety and professionalism expected of a law-enforcement aviation unit.
- **Pilots and drone operators are personally responsible for safe operations.** If you are in control of an aircraft or drone, the safety of that asset and everyone nearby is your responsibility.
- **Maintain realistic aviation and tactical RP at all times.** Every patrol, deployment, and callout must reflect genuine aviation law-enforcement procedures.
- AOD personnel are expected to maintain professionalism, activity standards, and operational readiness at all times.

<Warning>
  Failure to follow aviation procedures, reckless operation of aircraft or drones, or failure to meet activity and professionalism requirements may result in **immediate removal from the Air Operations Division**. Aviation assets represent some of the most significant resources the department operates — treat them accordingly.
</Warning>

## How to Apply

Reach out to an AOD supervisor or command member in-game or through the community Discord to register your interest. Ensure you meet the rank and activity requirements before approaching, and be prepared to demonstrate strong communication skills, operational awareness, and a commitment to professional aviation standards throughout the evaluation process.

<Tip>
  AOD looks for operators who understand that aviation is a support tool, not a spectacle. Show that you can identify *when* aerial assets add genuine value to an operation — that awareness is what separates a good AOD operator from a great one.
</Tip>
