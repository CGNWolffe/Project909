The Emergency Response Team (ERT) is the department's primary tactical unit, activated for the most dangerous and complex situations frontline patrol cannot safely handle alone. As an ERT operator, you respond to barricaded suspects, riots, high-risk warrant executions, and critical incidents where specialized tactical capability is essential. ERT demands a high standard of professionalism, discipline, and activity — membership is earned through a mandatory selection process and maintained through consistent performance.

## Division Overview

ERT bridges the gap between standard patrol and full tactical operations. When frontline units call for backup on an armed suspect, a barricade situation, or a complex warrant execution, ERT is the answer. You also conduct VIP and delegate escorts, support cross-border operations, and provide a rapid tactical response to any critical incident that develops during your shift. Even while standing by, you remain on active patrol and available to assist frontline officers at any time.

## Responsibilities

- Provide tactical support during high-risk incidents including armed confrontations and active threats
- Respond to barricaded suspects, civil disturbances, and riot situations
- Execute high-risk search warrants and tactical entry operations
- Conduct VIP and delegate protection escorts, including cross-border support
- Assist frontline patrol units during critical incidents requiring tactical capability
- Maintain standard patrol duties while on active shift and available for callouts
- Uphold professionalism, discipline, and tactical readiness at all times

## Qualifications

<Steps>
  <Step title="Meet the Rank Requirement">
    You must hold the rank of **Senior Constable or higher** to be eligible for ERT. Applications from lower-ranked members will not be considered.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Meet the Activity Standard">
    ERT requires a minimum of **3 days of active patrol per week**. Consistent activity demonstrates the commitment the unit demands.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Demonstrate Core Competencies">
    You must show strong report-writing ability, effective communication under pressure, and the professional maturity expected of a tactical operator. These are assessed throughout the process.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the ERT Tryout">
    All candidates must successfully pass a **mandatory 2-day tryout and selection process**. This evaluates your tactical ability, decision-making, teamwork, and composure under stress. There are no shortcuts or exceptions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Rules and Restrictions

ERT operates under strict operational rules. These standards protect both the integrity of the unit and the safety of every operator in the field.

- **Tactical vehicles are restricted to authorized ERT members only.** Unauthorized use is a direct policy violation.
- **Bearcat deployment requires supervisor or command approval** except when an immediate active threat demands immediate protective deployment.
- **Maintain realistic tactical RP at all times.** Every callout, entry, and use-of-force decision must reflect professional law enforcement standards.
- **No unauthorized tactical equipment or uniforms.** Wear only approved ERT gear during operations.
- **Tactical callouts take priority over standard patrol duties.** When a callout comes in, you respond — that is why you are on ERT.
- Maintain discipline, professionalism, and activity standards at all times.

<Warning>
  Failure to maintain professionalism, activity requirements, or operational standards may result in **removal from the Emergency Response Team**. All applicants and operators are expected to demonstrate tactical maturity and consistent performance.
</Warning>

## How to Apply

Express your interest to an ERT supervisor or command member in-game or through the community Discord. Ensure you meet the rank and activity requirements before approaching, and be prepared to demonstrate your patrol standards, report writing, and professional conduct.

<Note>
  The 2-day tryout selection process is mandatory for all candidates. There are no waivers or exceptions. Prepare yourself thoroughly — ERT leadership is evaluating your composure and decision-making as much as your tactical skills.
</Note>
