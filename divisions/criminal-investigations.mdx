The Gang Unit (GU) is the department's plainclothes enforcement and investigative arm for gang-related crime. As a Gang Unit deputy, you conduct covert surveillance, gather intelligence, execute search warrants, and build comprehensive case files on gang activity, including narcotics, weapons, and organized crime tied to it. The unit demands discretion, maturity, and an investigator's mindset in every situation you encounter.

## Unit Overview

Where patrol units respond to incidents as they happen, the Gang Unit works the threads that connect those incidents to larger gang patterns. You operate in plainclothes with unmarked vehicles, which means your ability to maintain operational security and confidentiality is as important as your investigative skills. The Gang Unit coordinates closely with frontline patrol units and ERT when operations require a tactical component, but the investigative lead always rests with the unit.

## Responsibilities

- Conduct gang investigations and manage follow-up case files to resolution
- Gather intelligence and collect evidence related to ongoing gang activity
- Execute search warrants and carry out enforcement operations in the field
- Conduct covert surveillance and authorized undercover operations
- Prepare detailed, accurate, and professional reports and case documentation
- Coordinate with patrol divisions and ERT during joint operations
- Assist with narcotics, weapons, and organized crime cases connected to gangs
- Maintain strict confidentiality and operational security throughout every investigation

## Qualifications

<Steps>
  <Step title="Meet the Rank Requirement">
    You must hold the rank of **Deputy Sheriff or higher** and have completed field training. This ensures you have the foundational patrol experience required for gang enforcement and investigative work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Meet the Activity Standard">
    The Gang Unit requires a minimum of **3 days of active operation per week**. Investigations are ongoing — your presence and contribution matter consistently.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Demonstrate Investigative Ability">
    You must show strong report-writing skills, investigative reasoning, and professional communication ability. Unit leadership reviews these capabilities throughout the application process.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pass the Gang Unit Evaluation and Interview">
    All candidates must complete a **Gang Unit evaluation and formal interview process**. This assesses your investigative aptitude, professional maturity, ability to maintain confidentiality, and operational security awareness.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Rules and Restrictions

The Gang Unit operates under strict rules that protect the integrity of every investigation and the safety of every operator in the field.

- **Unit vehicles are restricted to authorized Gang Unit members only.** Unauthorized use is a policy violation.
- **All undercover operations must remain confidential.** You do not share operational details outside of your investigation team and command unless required by procedure.
- **Maintain realistic investigative RP at all times.** Investigations must reflect genuine gang-unit work — shortcuts and unrealistic scenarios degrade the quality of every operation.
- **No police markings on undercover vehicles.** This is non-negotiable — operational security depends on it.
- **Detailed and accurate reports are mandatory.** Every investigation, surveillance, and warrant must be documented thoroughly.
- **Follow [Use of Force](/procedures/use-of-force) on every contact.** Gang affiliation alone never justifies a stop, search, or force.
- Professionalism and confidentiality are non-optional in the Gang Unit. Both are requirements, not guidelines.

<Warning>
  Unauthorized disclosure of active investigations, misuse of investigative equipment, or failure to maintain professional and confidentiality standards may result in **immediate removal from the Gang Unit**. The unit operates on trust — violating that trust has immediate consequences.
</Warning>

## How to Apply

Approach a Gang Unit supervisor or command member in-game or through the community Discord to express your interest. Ensure you meet the rank and activity requirements before making contact, and come prepared to demonstrate your investigative mindset, report quality, and professional conduct.

<Tip>
  Before applying, review your patrol reports. Gang Unit leadership will want to see that you already write thorough, accurate reports at the patrol level — that skill only becomes more critical inside the unit.
</Tip>
