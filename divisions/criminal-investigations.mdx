The Criminal Investigations Branch (CIB) is the department's plainclothes investigative arm, responsible for handling criminal cases that go beyond the scope of routine patrol. As a CIB detective, you conduct covert surveillance, gather intelligence, execute search warrants, and build comprehensive case files on serious criminal activity — from gang operations and narcotics to organized crime and major offences. CIB demands discretion, maturity, and an investigator's mindset in every situation you encounter.

## Division Overview

Where patrol units respond to incidents as they happen, CIB works the threads that connect those incidents to larger criminal patterns. You operate undercover, often in plainclothes with unmarked vehicles, which means your ability to maintain operational security and confidentiality is as important as your investigative skills. CIB coordinates closely with frontline patrol units and ERT when operations require a tactical component, but the investigative lead always rests with your branch.

## Responsibilities

- Conduct criminal investigations and manage follow-up case files to resolution
- Gather intelligence and collect evidence related to ongoing criminal activity
- Execute search warrants and carry out investigative operations in the field
- Conduct covert surveillance and authorized undercover operations
- Prepare detailed, accurate, and professional reports and case documentation
- Coordinate with patrol divisions and ERT during joint investigative operations
- Assist with major crimes, gang investigations, narcotics enforcement, and organized crime cases
- Maintain strict confidentiality and operational security throughout every investigation

## Qualifications

<Steps>
  <Step title="Meet the Rank Requirement">
    You must hold the rank of **Senior Constable or higher** to be eligible for CIB. This rank ensures you have the foundational patrol experience required for investigative work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Meet the Activity Standard">
    CIB requires a minimum of **3 days of active operation per week**. Investigations are ongoing — your presence and contribution matter consistently.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Demonstrate Investigative Ability">
    You must show strong report-writing skills, investigative reasoning, and professional communication ability. CIB reviews these capabilities throughout the application process.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pass the CIB Evaluation and Interview">
    All candidates must complete a **CIB evaluation and formal interview process**. This assesses your investigative aptitude, professional maturity, ability to maintain confidentiality, and operational security awareness.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Rules and Restrictions

CIB operates under strict rules that protect the integrity of every investigation and the safety of every operator in the field.

- **Investigative vehicles are restricted to authorized CIB members only.** Unauthorized use of CIB vehicles is a policy violation.
- **All undercover operations must remain confidential.** You do not share operational details outside of your investigation team and command unless required by procedure.
- **Maintain realistic investigative RP at all times.** Investigations must reflect genuine detective work — shortcuts and unrealistic scenarios degrade the quality of every CIB operation.
- **No police markings on undercover vehicles.** This is non-negotiable — operational security depends on it.
- **Detailed and accurate reports are mandatory.** Every investigation, surveillance, and warrant must be documented thoroughly.
- Professionalism and confidentiality are non-optional in CIB. Both are requirements, not guidelines.

<Warning>
  Unauthorized disclosure of active investigations, misuse of investigative equipment, or failure to maintain professional and confidentiality standards may result in **immediate removal from the Criminal Investigations Branch**. CIB operates on trust — violating that trust has immediate consequences.
</Warning>

## How to Apply

Approach a CIB supervisor or command member in-game or through the community Discord to express your interest. Ensure you meet the rank and activity requirements before making contact, and come prepared to demonstrate your investigative mindset, report quality, and professional conduct.

<Tip>
  Before applying, review your patrol reports. CIB leadership will want to see that you already write thorough, accurate reports at the patrol level — that skill only becomes more critical inside the branch.
</Tip>
