Project 909 is a FiveM law enforcement and fire department roleplay community set in San Bernardino County, California. The community is built around realistic, professional roleplay — covering everything from routine traffic enforcement on state highways to complex multi-unit emergency response scenes. Whether you are brand new to law enforcement roleplay or a seasoned veteran, Project 909 offers a structured environment where every member is expected to perform their role with dedication and realism.

## What Is Project 909?

Project 909 takes its name from the area code for San Bernardino County, California — the heart of the community's fictional jurisdiction. The server operates on FiveM, a multiplayer modification for Grand Theft Auto V, and focuses exclusively on law enforcement and emergency services roleplay. Members interact through a shared CAD system (Imperial CAD), structured radio communications, and a clear chain of command within each department.

The community emphasizes:

- **Realistic standards** — procedures mirror real-world law enforcement practices
- **Professionalism** — all members are held to a consistent code of conduct on and off duty
- **Structure** — ranks, divisions, and chain of command are actively maintained
- **Collaboration** — departments and units coordinate through dispatch and shared communications

## Available Departments

Project 909 currently supports three primary departments, each with its own structure, ranks, and operational responsibilities.

### San Bernardino County Sheriff's Department (SBCSD)

The SBCSD is the primary law enforcement agency within the community. Deputies handle patrol operations, traffic enforcement, criminal investigations, and community response across the county. The department includes several specialized divisions for members who meet the requirements.

SBCSD patrol operations are organized by station, each covering a distinct area of the county:

- **Rancho Cucamonga Station** — covers the west end of the county. High call volume, dense urban and suburban patrol, heavy retail and commercial districts, and frequent traffic enforcement along the I-15 and Foothill corridors.
- **Victorville Station** — covers the High Desert. Larger patrol area with longer response times, a mix of urban centers and open desert highway, and significant coverage along I-15 north.

Deputies are assigned to a station during onboarding and may request a transfer through their chain of command.

### California Highway Patrol (CHP)

The CHP focuses on highway and freeway enforcement across the county's major corridors. CHP officers specialize in traffic stops, vehicle pursuit policy, DUI enforcement, and collision investigation. Members who join the CHP are expected to maintain a high standard of traffic law knowledge and patrol conduct.

### Fire Department

The Project 909 Fire Department handles emergency medical services, structure fires, vehicle accidents, and other emergency response calls. Fire department members work closely with law enforcement units and dispatch to coordinate multi-agency responses.

## Community Structure

### Ranks

Each department uses its own rank structure. New members begin at the entry-level rank and advance through demonstrated performance, activity, and supervisor recommendation.

**San Bernardino County Sheriff's Department**

- **Deputy Sheriff Trainee** — newly hired, completing field training
- **Deputy Sheriff** — full patrol deputy after FTO sign-off
- **Corporal** — senior deputy with training and shift-lead responsibilities
- **Detective** — investigative assignment
- **Sergeant** — first-line supervisor
- **Lieutenant and above** — station and command staff

**California Highway Patrol**

- **Cadet** — in training
- **Officer** — full patrol officer after FTO sign-off
- **Sergeant** — first-line supervisor
- **Lieutenant and above** — area and command staff

**Fire Department**

- **Probationary Firefighter** — newly hired, completing probation
- **Firefighter / Firefighter-Paramedic** — full duty status
- **Engineer** — apparatus operator
- **Captain** — company officer
- **Battalion Chief and above** — command staff

Rank advancement is not automatic. Supervisors review activity, conduct, and skill before endorsing a promotion.

### Divisions

Beyond standard patrol, several specialized divisions are available to members who meet the eligibility requirements. These include the Highway Safety Division, Emergency Response Team, and others. Division membership carries additional activity requirements and expectations.

### Dispatch

Dispatch operates as a coordination layer between all active units. All members — whether on patrol or in a specialized unit — communicate through dispatch using Imperial CAD and the community's radio system. Dispatch monitors unit statuses, assigns calls, and ensures safe unit deployment across the county.
