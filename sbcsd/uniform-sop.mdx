SBCSD members are expected to present a professional, uniform appearance any time they are on duty. These standards are modeled after real-world sheriff's department dress and grooming policy to keep the community grounded and consistent.

## Standard Duty Uniform (Class D)

The Class D uniform is the standard patrol uniform worn on regular shifts.

- Department-issued uniform shirt (short or long sleeve) with SBCSD shoulder patches on both sleeves
- Duty trousers, department-approved color, worn with a black leather or leather-look duty belt
- Black boots or duty shoes, clean and in good repair
- Badge worn centered on the left breast; nameplate on the right breast
- Duty belt configured with holster, magazine pouches, cuff case, radio holder, and any other issued equipment
- Ballistic vest worn under the uniform shirt at all times while on patrol

<Warning>
  Members may not modify, mix components with, or substitute non-issued items into the standard uniform without supervisor approval.
</Warning>

## Rank Insignia and Badges

- Rank insignia is worn on the collar or shoulder as issued, consistent with the member's current rank (see [Ranks](/sbcsd/ranks)).
- The badge and department patches must be Department-issued and worn in the correct position at all times while in uniform.
- Undersized, damaged, or unauthorized badges/patches are not permitted.

## Equipment and Gear

- All less-lethal and lethal equipment must be carried in the position it was trained and approved for.
- Portable radios must be worn in a visible, accessible position — not buried in a pocket or bag.
- Body cameras, where issued, must be worn and activated per [Patrol Procedures](/procedures/patrol).
- Personal add-ons (patches, pins, stickers) beyond what's issued are not permitted on the uniform.

## Grooming Standards

Personal hygiene and a neat appearance are considered part of the uniform. Supervisors may send a member home to correct their appearance if standards aren't met.

- Hair must be neat and, if long, restrained or pinned so it doesn't interfere with equipment (helmet, headset, gas mask) or present a safety hazard.
- Hair coloring should stay within a natural range.
- Fingernails must be kept trim and clean.
- Jewelry must be minimal and may not create a safety hazard — no dangling earrings, and only small, non-facial piercings are permitted while in uniform.

### Facial Hair

- **Mustaches** are permitted and must be neatly trimmed, not extending past the corners of the mouth or below the lip line.
- **Beards** are permitted for patrol members provided they are neatly and evenly trimmed, kept short (roughly a quarter-inch to half-inch in length), and do not interfere with the seal of a gas mask or other protective equipment.
- Patchy, unshaped, or unkempt facial hair is not considered compliant and should be either fully grown out to a uniform length or shaved.
- Sideburns must be neatly trimmed and kept to a conservative length, not extending below the earlobe.
- Supervisors may require a member to trim or shave if facial hair interferes with safety equipment or fails to meet a neat, professional standard.

### Tattoos

- Tattoos on the **head, face, or neck** must be fully covered at all times while on duty, using a long-sleeve undershirt collar, neck gaiter, or skin-toned covering as appropriate — they may never be left visible.
- Tattoos elsewhere on the body **may be visible** while in uniform, provided they don't depict or reference gang activity, hate symbols, profanity, drug use, or sexually explicit imagery.
- Any tattoo that violates the above must be fully covered with a long sleeve, sleeve wrap, or skin-toned patch before going on duty.
- Members should make a reasonable effort to keep tattoos covered when working a scene with heavy public interaction, at a supervisor's discretion.

<Note>
  When in doubt about whether a tattoo, beard length, or piece of equipment is compliant, ask your supervisor before going 10-8. It's faster to fix at sign-on than mid-shift.
</Note>

## Class B (Formal/Court Attire)

For court appearances, promotional ceremonies, or other formal Department functions:

- Male members: dress uniform or a business suit and tie, per Department direction
- Female members: dress uniform, business suit, or conservative dress attire
- All tattoos must be fully covered for court appearances, regardless of location on the body
- Duty cap or dress cap may be required depending on the event
