CHP members are expected to present a professional, uniform appearance any time they are on duty. These standards are modeled after real-world highway patrol dress and grooming policy.

## Standard Patrol Uniform (Class B)

The Class B uniform is the standard patrol uniform worn on regular shifts.

- Tan uniform shirt (short or long sleeve) with CHP shoulder patches on both sleeves
- Tan duty trousers with the blue and gold side stripe
- Black duty belt with holster, magazine pouches, cuff case, and radio holder
- Black boots or duty shoes, clean and in good repair
- Badge worn on the left breast; nameplate on the right breast
- Ballistic vest worn under the uniform shirt at all times while on patrol

## Optional and Situational Items

| Item | When worn |
| --- | --- |
| Department jacket | Cold weather or night patrol |
| High-visibility vest | **Required** when working collision scenes or directing traffic outside your vehicle |
| Duty cap | Optional on patrol; required for formal functions |
| Motor helmet, boots, and breeches | Motor Unit officers only |

<Warning>
  Members may not modify, mix components with, or substitute non-issued items into the uniform without supervisor approval.
</Warning>

## Rank Insignia and Badges

- Rank insignia is worn on the collar as issued, matching your current rank (see [Ranks](/chp/ranks)).
- Badges and patches must be department-issued and worn in the correct position at all times while in uniform.
- Division patches (CVE, K9, Motors) may only be worn by members currently assigned to that division.

## Equipment and Gear

- Carry all lethal and less-lethal equipment in the position it was trained and approved for.
- Wear your portable radio in a visible, accessible position.
- Body cameras, where issued, must be worn and activated per [Patrol Procedures](/procedures/patrol).
- Personal add-ons (patches, pins, stickers) beyond what's issued are not permitted.

## Grooming Standards

- Hair must be neat and, if long, restrained so it doesn't interfere with equipment or present a safety hazard. Coloring should stay within a natural range.
- Mustaches and short, neatly trimmed beards are permitted as long as they don't interfere with protective equipment. Patchy or unkempt facial hair is not compliant.
- Jewelry must be minimal — no dangling earrings or facial piercings while in uniform.
- Tattoos on the head, face, or neck must be covered while on duty. Tattoos elsewhere may be visible unless they show gang, hate, drug, profane, or sexually explicit content.

<Note>
  When in doubt about whether something is compliant, ask your supervisor before going 10-8.
</Note>

## Class A (Formal/Court Attire)

For court appearances, promotional ceremonies, and other formal functions:

- Full dress uniform with duty cap, or business attire per department direction
- All tattoos must be covered, regardless of location
