CHP patrol vehicles are department property and must be operated safely and only by authorized members. Select a tab below for each vehicle class.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Patrol (Black & White)">
    Marked black-and-white units are the standard CHP patrol vehicle, used for highway patrol, traffic stops, and collision response.

    | Vehicle | Type | Access |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | 2025 Ford Explorer | Marked | All officers |
    | 2025 Dodge Charger | Marked | All officers |
    | 2024 Chevrolet Tahoe | Marked | Officers and supervisors |

    **Cadets** may only operate a patrol unit while riding with their FTO.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Slick Top / Low Profile">
    Slick-top and low-profile units have no roof lightbar and are used for targeted traffic enforcement.

    | Vehicle | Type | Access |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | 2025 Dodge Charger | Slick Top | Officers with supervisor approval |
    | 2025 Ford Explorer | Slick Top | Officers with supervisor approval |

    <Warning>
      Slick-top units are for proactive traffic enforcement only, not routine patrol.
    </Warning>
  </Tab>

## Vehicle Rules

- Only operate vehicles you are authorized for. Using a division or supervisor vehicle without approval is a policy violation.
- Drive with due regard for public safety at all times, including during code 3 responses.
- Inspect your vehicle at sign-on and report damage or equipment issues to a supervisor.
- Return vehicles to standard at the end of your shift.

<Warning>
  Unauthorized vehicle use or reckless driving may result in loss of vehicle privileges, suspension, or removal from the department.
</Warning>
