The CHP K9 Unit pairs certified handlers with trained canines to support highway patrol operations. Where the SBCSD [K9 Division](/divisions/k9) focuses on tracking and area searches, CHP K9 concentrates on **narcotics detection and highway interdiction** — vehicle sniffs during traffic stops on the county's freeway corridors — plus suspect apprehension after pursuits. Handlers are accountable for every deployment their dog makes.

## Division Overview

CHP K9 units respond when patrol officers need a narcotics sniff on a stopped vehicle, a suspect search after a bailout, or K9 support on a high-risk stop. You and your canine operate as a team at all times, and every deployment must be justified and documented.

## Responsibilities

- Conduct exterior vehicle sniffs during lawful traffic stops
- Support highway interdiction operations on major corridors
- Track and apprehend suspects who flee on foot after a pursuit or stop
- Assist on felony and high-risk stops when a K9 capability is needed
- Maintain positive control of your canine at all times
- Complete a report for every K9 deployment
- Keep the K9 vehicle and equipment in operational condition

## Qualifications

- Must be an **Officer** in good standing and a **certified K9 handler** approved by K9 leadership
- Must understand the legal limits on vehicle sniffs and K9 use of force
- Strong traffic stop fundamentals and report writing

## Operations

### Vehicle Sniffs

- A sniff may only happen during a **lawful stop**. You may not hold a driver longer than the stop requires just to wait for a K9 unless you have separate reasonable suspicion.
- Sniffs are conducted on the **exterior** of the vehicle. Occupants should be clear of the vehicle or safely positioned before the dog works.
- An alert from a certified dog can provide probable cause to search. Document the alert, where it happened, and what was found.

### Apprehensions

- Give a clear K9 warning announcement before deploying for an apprehension whenever it is safe to do so.
- Deploy for a bite only when the suspect's actions justify that level of force under [Use of Force](/procedures/use-of-force).
- Call the dog off as soon as the suspect is under control, and request EMS for any bite.

## Rules and Restrictions

- **K9 vehicles are for certified handlers only.**
- **Every deployment must be lawful and justified.** An unjustified bite is a use-of-force violation.
- **Don't extend a stop just to get a sniff** without separate reasonable suspicion.
- **You are responsible for your dog at all times.**
- Document every deployment.

<Warning>
  Unauthorized K9 use or improper deployments may result in **immediate removal from the K9 Unit**.
</Warning>

## How to Apply

Speak with a CHP K9 supervisor or command member in-game or through the community Discord. Show strong traffic stop fundamentals and a clear understanding of search and seizure and use-of-force rules.

<Tip>
  Knowing when a sniff is *not* lawful is just as important as running one. Leadership looks for handlers who respect those limits.
</Tip>
