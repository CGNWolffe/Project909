The CHP K9 Unit pairs certified handlers with trained canines to support highway patrol operations. Where the SBCSD [K9 Division](/divisions/k9) focuses on tracking and area searches, CHP K9 concentrates on **narcotics detection and highway interdiction**, meaning vehicle sniffs during traffic stops on the county's freeway corridors, plus suspect apprehension after pursuits. Handlers are accountable for every deployment their dog makes.

<Info>
  This page is a Project 909 roleplay SOP adapted from standard California law enforcement canine practice. It is not a copy of any real agency's policy. CHP K9 follows the same deployment, announcement, call-off, medical, and reporting rules as the [SBCSD K9 Division](/divisions/k9). This page adds the highway-specific rules. Where it overlaps with [Use of Force](/procedures/use-of-force), the stricter rule applies.
</Info>

## Purpose and authority

- K9 deployment is a use of force, and a vehicle sniff is a search. Both must be lawful and justified.
- You are in command of your dog. Nobody else gives it commands or handles it.
- Supervisor approval authorizes a deployment. It never transfers your responsibility for it.

## Handler selection and certification

<Steps>
  <Step title="Meet the prerequisites">
    You must be an **Officer** in good standing with strong traffic stop fundamentals and report writing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply to K9 leadership">
    Speak with a CHP K9 supervisor or command member in-game or through the community Discord. Show that you understand search and seizure and use-of-force rules.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete K9 training">
    Training covers handler control, lawful sniffs, deployment criteria, announcements, call-off discipline, and K9 reporting. K9 leadership signs you off as a **certified K9 handler**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stay current">
    Handlers who are inactive, or who show poor control or judgment, may be re-evaluated or removed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Membership in the K9 Unit is separate from rank. Officers keep their patrol rank while assigned (see [Ranks](/chp/ranks)).

## Responsibilities

- Conduct exterior vehicle sniffs during lawful traffic stops
- Support highway interdiction operations on major corridors
- Track and apprehend suspects who flee on foot after a pursuit or stop
- Assist on felony and high-risk stops when a K9 capability is needed
- Keep positive control of your canine at all times
- Report every deployment, including sniffs and deployments that ended without a bite
- Keep the K9 vehicle and equipment in service condition

## Vehicle sniffs

### Lawful basis

- A sniff may only happen during a **lawful stop**.
- You may not hold a driver longer than the stop requires just to wait for a K9 unless you have separate reasonable suspicion.
- Sniffing a vehicle you have no lawful reason to stop is not permitted.

### Conducting a sniff

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm the stop is still lawful">
    The stop must still be within its original purpose and duration. If the stop's purpose is complete, you need separate reasonable suspicion to continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear the vehicle">
    Have the occupants exit or position them safely away from the vehicle before the dog works. Keep other officers out of the dog's path.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sniff the exterior only">
    The dog works the **exterior** of the vehicle. Do not let the dog enter the vehicle unless you have lawful authority to search it and it is safe to do so.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Act on the alert">
    An alert from a certified dog can provide probable cause to search. State the alert on the radio, then conduct the search.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  A sniff that extends a stop without separate reasonable suspicion is an unlawful detention, and anything found may be unusable. Report it as a violation if it happens.
</Warning>

## Apprehensions

Apprehension deployments follow the SBCSD K9 SOP in full:

- [When K9 is and is not appropriate](/divisions/k9#when-k9-is-appropriate)
- [Request and authorization](/divisions/k9#request-and-authorization), including **explicit supervisor approval** logged in Imperial CAD
- [Announcement and release](/divisions/k9#announcement-and-release), using the CHP agency name in your announcement
- [Call-off](/divisions/k9#apprehension-and-call-off) the moment the suspect stops resisting

Highway-specific rules:

- **Stop traffic first.** Request lane closures or traffic control before a search or release near active lanes. Never release a dog toward live traffic.
- **Check the shoulder and median.** Keep bystanders, motorists, and other units clear of the search area.
- **Treat a bailout as contained only after a perimeter exists.** Do not start a track into unknown terrain without backup.

## Medical aid and reporting

Request EMS for **every bite or injury**, give aid until EMS takes over, and report every deployment. See [Medical aid](/divisions/k9#medical-aid) and [Reporting and review](/divisions/k9#reporting-and-review) for the full requirements.

For a sniff, also record:

| Include | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Basis for the stop** | Why the stop was lawful and how long it lasted |
| **Basis for the sniff** | That the sniff did not extend the stop, or the separate reasonable suspicion |
| **Alert** | Where on the vehicle the dog alerted |
| **Search results** | What was found and where |

## Rules and restrictions

- **K9 vehicles are for certified handlers only.**
- **Every deployment must be lawful, justified, and approved.** An unjustified bite is a use-of-force violation.
- **Never extend a stop just to get a sniff** without separate reasonable suspicion.
- **Never release on a compliant or surrendering subject.**
- **Request EMS for every bite.**
- **You control your dog at all times.**
- **Report every deployment.**

<Warning>
  Unauthorized K9 use or improper deployment may result in **immediate removal from the K9 Unit**.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Knowing when a sniff is *not* lawful is just as important as running one. Leadership looks for handlers who respect those limits.
</Tip>
