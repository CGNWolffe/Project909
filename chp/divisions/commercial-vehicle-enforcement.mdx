The CHP Commercial Vehicle Enforcement unit (CVE) keeps commercial trucks, buses, and freight traffic safe and compliant on the state highways running through San Bernardino County. The I-15 through the Cajon Pass and the I-10 through the Inland Empire carry some of the heaviest truck traffic in the state. CVE officers handle the stops and inspections general patrol isn't equipped for: equipment violations, overweight loads, unsecured cargo, hazardous materials, and driver hours of service.

## Division Overview

CVE officers focus on commercial motor vehicles (CMVs) — any truck, tractor-trailer, bus, or vehicle carrying passengers or freight for commercial purposes. You identify unsafe commercial vehicles, run roadside inspections and weigh stations, place dangerous vehicles or drivers out of service, and support collision response when a commercial vehicle is involved. CVE works alongside the SBCSD [Commercial Vehicle Enforcement](/divisions/commercial-vehicle-enforcement) unit on county roads.

## Responsibilities

- Conduct traffic stops and roadside inspections on commercial motor vehicles
- Staff weigh stations (scale houses) and run checkpoint operations
- Inspect equipment, cargo securement, and driver documentation
- Enforce weight, size, and permit restrictions on state highways
- Identify and manage hazardous materials (HAZMAT) loads during stops and collisions
- Issue out-of-service orders for unsafe vehicles or drivers
- Investigate collisions involving commercial vehicles
- Complete detailed inspection and enforcement reports for every CVE contact

## Qualifications

- Must be an **Officer** in good standing and authorized by CVE leadership
- Must show consistent, realistic traffic enforcement RP on general patrol
- Must understand inspection levels and out-of-service criteria before working solo
- Supervisor approval required to open a weigh station or run a checkpoint

## Operations

### Commercial Vehicle Stops

Commercial vehicles are large, heavy, and slow to stop. Plan your stop before you light them up.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a safe location">
    Use a wide shoulder, off-ramp, truck stop, or open weigh station with room for the full length of the vehicle. Avoid curves, grades, bridges, and narrow shoulders.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Call it out">
    Advise Dispatch of your route, direction, nearest exit or mile marker, the vehicle type, company name or DOT number if visible, and the plate. Note any HAZMAT placards.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Position your unit">
    Park offset behind the trailer so you are visible to traffic and clear of the truck's blind spots. Keep emergency lighting on for the full stop.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make contact">
    Approach on the passenger side when traffic allows. Have the driver set the parking brake and shut off the engine.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect documents">
    Request the CDL, medical card, registration, insurance, bill of lading or shipping papers, and logbook or ELD record.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Roadside Inspections

| Level | Name | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Level I** | Full Inspection | Driver documents plus a full walk-around and under-vehicle check of brakes, tires, lights, coupling, frame, and cargo securement |
| **Level II** | Walk-Around | Driver documents and a walk-around check of everything visible without going under the vehicle |
| **Level III** | Driver Only | CDL, medical card, hours of service, and shipping papers — no vehicle check |
| **Level IV** | Special | A one-time check of a single item, such as a specific equipment complaint |

<Note>
  Level I inspections take time. Request a second unit for traffic control when working a full inspection on a busy freeway.
</Note>

### Weigh Stations

- Weigh stations open only with supervisor approval. Advise Dispatch when a scale opens and closes.
- Direct trucks into the scale lane and weigh each axle group and the gross weight.
- Trucks that bypass an open scale may be stopped and brought back.
- Significantly overweight trucks stay at the scale until the load is reduced or redistributed.
- Oversize or overweight loads must carry a valid permit and stay on the approved route.

### Hazardous Materials

- Note the placard number and class on the radio as soon as you see it.
- Keep smoking and open flame away from the vehicle.
- If the load is leaking, damaged, or involved in a collision, stay upwind and uphill, request **Fire and a HAZMAT response**, and close the area.
- Never move, open, or clean up a hazardous load yourself.

<Warning>
  Treat any unknown leak or spill from a commercial vehicle as hazardous until Fire confirms otherwise.
</Warning>

### Out-of-Service Orders

An out-of-service (OOS) order means the vehicle or driver may not continue until the problem is fixed. Issue one when you find:

- **Vehicle:** failed or out-of-adjustment brakes, flat or badly damaged tires, broken coupling devices, unsecured or shifting cargo, or missing required lighting at night
- **Driver:** no valid CDL, expired or missing medical card, over the legal hours of service, or signs of impairment

The vehicle stays parked until it is repaired, towed, or a qualified replacement driver arrives. Document the reason and outcome in your report.

### Commercial Vehicle Collisions

- Shield the scene and request extra units for traffic control — truck collisions often block multiple lanes.
- Check for HAZMAT placards before approaching.
- Request Fire and EMS for injuries, fuel leaks, or spilled loads, plus a heavy-duty tow.
- Once the scene is safe, inspect the commercial vehicle for equipment failures that may have caused the collision.

## Rules and Restrictions

- **CVE vehicles are restricted to CVE members only.**
- **Only stop commercial vehicles at safe locations.** Never stop a loaded truck where it can't pull fully off the road.
- **Every inspection needs a reason** — a violation, an equipment issue, or an approved checkpoint or open scale.
- **Weigh stations and checkpoints require supervisor approval.**
- **Do not handle HAZMAT loads yourself.**
- Document every stop, inspection, and OOS order.

<Warning>
  Unauthorized use of CVE vehicles, unsafe stops, or inspections without a lawful reason may result in disciplinary action or **removal from CVE**.
</Warning>
