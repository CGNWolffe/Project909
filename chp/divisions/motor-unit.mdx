The CHP Motor Unit puts officers on police motorcycles for traffic enforcement on the county's highways. Motors can move through congestion, reach collisions in stopped traffic, and run targeted enforcement where a patrol car can't easily get to. Riding a motor takes extra skill and carries extra risk, so the standards are high.

## Division Overview

Motor officers focus on traffic: speed and moving-violation enforcement, collision response in heavy traffic, traffic breaks, and escort details. Motors are **not** used for pursuits or high-risk stops — those go to four-wheel units.

## Responsibilities

- Run targeted traffic enforcement on freeways and highways
- Respond to collisions and hazards in heavy or stopped traffic
- Run traffic breaks and assist with lane closures
- Provide escorts for funerals, oversize loads, and approved events
- Complete reports for every stop and incident

## Qualifications

- Must be an **Officer** in good standing, approved by Motor Unit leadership
- Must complete Motor Unit riding training and sign-off before riding solo
- Strong traffic enforcement fundamentals

## Operations

- **Stops:** Pick a location with a wide shoulder. Park the motorcycle angled behind the violator and keep emergency lighting on. Request a four-wheel unit for any arrest or transport.
- **Pursuits:** Motors may not initiate or join a pursuit. Call it out, give direction of travel, and let four-wheel units take it.
- **High-risk stops:** Do not conduct felony or high-risk stops on a motorcycle. Hold back and wait for cover units.
- **Weather:** Motors return to station or switch to a car in rain or other unsafe conditions.

## Rules and Restrictions

- **Motorcycles are restricted to Motor Unit members only.**
- **No pursuits and no high-risk stops** on a motorcycle.
- **Full motor gear at all times** while riding.
- Ride with due regard for public safety, including when lane splitting through traffic.

<Warning>
  Reckless riding or unauthorized use of a Motor Unit motorcycle may result in **removal from the Motor Unit**.
</Warning>

## How to Apply

Speak with a Motor Unit supervisor or CHP command member in-game or through the community Discord. Show consistent, safe driving and strong traffic enforcement on general patrol before applying.
