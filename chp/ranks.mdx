New officers begin as Cadets and advance through demonstrated performance, activity, and supervisor recommendation.

## Rank Progression

| Rank | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Cadet** | In training, completing field training with an FTO |
| **Officer** | Full patrol officer after FTO sign-off |
| **Sergeant** | First-line supervisor; runs a shift and approves reports |
| **Lieutenant and above** | Area and command staff |

## Advancement

Rank advancement is not automatic. Supervisors review activity, conduct, and skill before endorsing a promotion. Being eligible for a rank does not entitle you to it — promotions depend on department need as well as individual performance.

<Note>
  Specialized division membership (CVE, K9, Motors) is separate from rank. Officers keep their patrol rank while assigned to a division.
</Note>
