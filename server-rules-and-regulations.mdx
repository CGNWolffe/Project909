Welcome to Project 909 Roleplay. This page sets out the official rules and regulations that govern our Discord server and the wider community. They apply in every text channel, every voice channel, and every interaction between members representing Project 909 — in addition to the in-character conduct standards covered on the [Department Rules](/rules-and-expectations) page.

<Warning>
  Not having read these rules is never a valid excuse for breaking them. Every member is responsible for knowing and following this page from the moment they join the server.
</Warning>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Eligibility & Account Standards">
    - You must be at least 16 years old to join Project 909. Staff may request age verification if there is reasonable doubt.
    - Your Discord account must meet Discord's own Terms of Service, including its minimum age requirement.
    - One Discord account and one in-game character set per person. Alternate accounts created to evade a ban, mute, or activity check are not permitted.
    - Set a real, recognizable display name and avatar. Do not impersonate staff, other members, real public figures, or fictional characters in a way that could mislead others.
    - Enabling two-factor authentication on your Discord account is strongly recommended to protect your membership from compromise.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Communication & Channel Conduct">
    - Treat every member with respect. Harassment, hate speech, discrimination, and personal attacks of any kind are not tolerated, in-character or out-of-character.
    - Use each channel for its intended purpose. Keep in-character chatter, memes, and casual conversation out of operational and announcement channels.
    - No spamming — this includes repeated messages, excessive emoji or sticker use, message walls, and unnecessary @everyone, @here, or role pings.
    - No NSFW, gory, or graphic content anywhere on the server, including profile pictures, statuses, and attachments.
    - No advertising other servers, communities, or personal projects without prior written approval from server leadership.
    - Keep divisive political and religious debate out of Project 909. This is a roleplay community, not a debate stage.
    - Direct messages between members are not moderated by staff, but harassment reported from DMs will still be actioned.

    <Tip>
      If a conversation is heating up, step away and open a staff ticket rather than letting it escalate in an open channel.
    </Tip>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Fair-Play Roleplay Regulations">
    These regulations protect the integrity of every scene. They apply on top of the department-specific conduct rules covered on the [Department Rules](/rules-and-expectations) page.

    - **Metagaming** — Using information your character could not realistically know (from Discord, streams, or another player) to influence a roleplay scenario is prohibited.
    - **Powergaming** — Forcing an outcome on another player without giving them a realistic chance to react (for example, an inescapable scripted action) is prohibited.
    - **Fail RP** — Roleplaying in a way that is unrealistic, breaks immersion, or ignores the natural consequences of a scene — including refusing to comply with a valid arrest scenario without justification — is not permitted.
    - **New Life Rule (NLR)** — Characters who are downed or killed in a scene must treat that event as it would realistically occur and cannot return to the same scene or seek revenge with knowledge of what happened.
    - **RDM & VDM** — Random Death Match (attacking another player without in-character justification) and Vehicle Death Match (using a vehicle to harm another player without justification) are serious violations.
    - **Combat Logging** — Disconnecting or leaving a scene to avoid arrest, death, or another in-progress roleplay consequence is prohibited.
    - **Exploiting** — Using bugs, glitches, or unintended game mechanics for personal or department advantage must be reported, not used.

    <Warning>
      RDM, VDM, combat logging, and exploiting are treated as serious violations and can result in an immediate suspension pending staff review, even on a first offence.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Staff Authority, Reporting & Appeals">
    - Staff decisions made during an active scene or incident are final at the time they are made. If you disagree, comply first and raise it through a ticket afterward.
    - Do not argue with, publicly criticise, or attempt to pressure staff in open channels. Raise concerns privately and respectfully.
    - Report rule violations using the staff ticket system, including any available evidence such as clips, screenshots, or timestamps. Do not attempt to handle serious violations yourself.
    - False reports made in bad faith, or repeated reports of a trivial nature, are themselves a violation of these regulations.
    - All disciplinary decisions may be appealed through the staff ticket system within 72 hours of the action being issued.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Enforcement & Strike System

Project 909 uses a structured strike system to keep enforcement consistent and predictable. Strikes are cumulative and do not expire automatically — they are reviewed by staff on a case-by-case basis.

| Strike | Typical Response |
| --- | --- |
| **1st Strike** | Verbal or written warning, logged to your member record. |
| **2nd Strike** | Formal written warning and a temporary mute or suspension from duty. |
| **3rd Strike** | Extended suspension from the server and a probationary period upon return. |
| **4th Strike** | Removal from the community. |

<Note>
  Some violations bypass this progression entirely. Severe misconduct — including hate speech, doxxing, sharing private community material, or impersonating staff — can result in an immediate and permanent removal regardless of prior history.
</Note>
